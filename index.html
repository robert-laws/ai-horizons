Q2: Integrating AI into Courses

How would you help faculty meaningfully integrate AI into teaching and assignments?

Sample Response Customize

I begin with a collaborative needs assessment and a 30-minute consult to map course outcomes, student readiness, and policy boundaries. Then I co-design a low-stakes pilot, such as AI-assisted brainstorming with explicit disclosure, reflection prompts, and rubric alignment.

Workflow

  1. Assess goals, constraints, and disciplinary context.
  2. Prototype one bounded activity in the LMS.
  3. Evaluate outcomes with faculty and student feedback.
  4. Scale only after integrity, accessibility, and equity checks.

Q4: Teaching AI to Skeptics and AI Literacy

How do you teach practical AI skills to skeptical colleagues and explain why AI literacy matters?

Sample Response Customize

With skeptics, I start with analogies and one low-risk hands-on example connected to current pain points. AI literacy is the ability to use, evaluate, and communicate AI responsibly. It matters because it supports equity, academic integrity, and informed innovation for every discipline.
